About

This calculator was made by Santosh M., a computer science student, to help students work out SGPA, CGPA, percentage and degree class using their own university rules.

Many calculators use one fixed grade table. Universities differ, so this tool lets you pick a profile or enter your own rules.

It is free to use. If you find a mistake or want your university added, please contact us.
