How to use this calculator

Choose your grading profile, add each subject with its credits and grade (or marks), and the result updates at once. If your college gives no credits, write 1 for every subject.

How it is calculated

SGPA = sum of (grade point × credits) ÷ total credits for a semester. CGPA does the same across all semesters, with each semester multiplied by its weight. Repeated subjects follow the retake rule you pick.

Frequently asked questions

What is a credit? A credit shows the weight of a subject. A 4 credit subject counts twice as much as a 2 credit subject.

My university ignores Year 1. Set the weight of Year 1 to 0 and it will not count.

What does 2:2 mean? In the UK, 70% and above is a First, 60 to 69 is a 2:1, 50 to 59 is a 2:2, and 40 to 49 is a Third.

My university is not in the list. Open My university rules, enter the grade points and class bands from your handbook, and press Save and use.

Is my data uploaded? No. It stays in your browser on your device. Use Copy my data to keep a backup.

Disclaimer
